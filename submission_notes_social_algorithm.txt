For the Week 7 assignment "Crack Social Media Algorithm" (Phase: Build, Workload: 3h), I deconstructed LinkedIn's 2026 organic algorithm specifically for FlyRank (B2B SaaS, AEO/GEO engine) and built a complete native content pack engineered around the platform's core distribution mechanics.

Key Strategic & Algorithmic Highlights:
1. Algorithmic Mechanics & Scoring Signals:
   - Dwell Time: Dwell-on-feed vs. dwell-on-expanded-content ("see more" clicks and multi-slide carousel reads) acts as LinkedIn's primary Stage 2/Stage 4 distribution trigger.
   - Comment Depth: 15+ word substantive comments receive 4x–6x the algorithmic weight of a generic reaction; author replies during the "Golden Hour" (first 60 minutes) act as a secondary distribution multiplier.
   - Document Swipes: PDF carousels register recursive engagement events per slide swipe.
   - Saves & Thoughtful Reposts: Weighted heavily as signals of long-term professional utility.

2. Penalties, Shadowbans & Compliance Guardrails:
   - The External Link Penalty: External links in the main post body incur an immediate 40–50% reach penalty. Solved by delivering 100% native value and placing links in the pinned first comment.
   - The 15-Minute Edit Penalty: Editing immediately post-publish resets micro-testing velocity.
   - FTC AI & B2B Compliance: Claims on AI performance are tied strictly to verifiable client benchmarks (Praella 5.2x growth, 120,700 URL study) rather than unsubstantiated guarantees.

3. Monetization Engine:
   - Tailored to high-ACV enterprise SaaS acquisition ($2,500–$10,000/month retainers) driven by profile funneling and the Free 60-Second AI Citation Audit lead magnet.

4. Sample Content Pack (Built to Win the Algorithm):
   - Asset #1: High-Dwell PDF Document Carousel (8-slide blueprint on "The 18.9% Age Gap", designed for >75s dwell time and high bookmark rates).
   - Asset #2: Contrarian Long-Form Text Post ("Stop paying freelance writers $400 for 3,000-word posts", triggering "see more" expansion, high vertical whitespace, and practitioner comment debates).
   - Asset #3: Social Proof / Case Study Framework ("How Praella tripled inbound pipeline without writing new posts", driving profile visits and inbound enterprise demo requests).
   - Each asset includes a granular "Why this fits the algorithm" justification.
