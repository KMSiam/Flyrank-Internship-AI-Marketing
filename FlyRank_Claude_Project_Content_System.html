The Power of Standing Project Instructions

Starting from a blank prompt creates generic, cliché-ridden AI copy full of prohibited health claims. By locking the Cymbiotika Master Brief into a persistent Claude Project, we define standing audience personas, sensory tone guidelines, banned words, and DSHEA compliance rules once. A single seed idea—"Why 80% of standard Vitamin C is flushed unabsorbed"—instantly blossoms into a week of high-converting, cross-channel assets.

1. The Claude Project Master Brief & System Rules

Voice & Tone Card

  • Clinically Credible yet Plainspoken: Explains biochemistry in sensory, accessible language.
  • Calm Authority: Zero hype, zero late-night infomercial tropes. Confidence from formulation.
  • Sensory & Grounded: Describes textures, cold-pressed citrus oils, and cellular uptake.

DSHEA & FDA Compliance Rules

  • Prohibited Disease Claims: Never say "cures colds", "treats illness", or "prevents disease".
  • Approved Structure/Function Language: "Supports natural cellular defense", "promotes natural energy", "enhances bioavailability".
  • Banned AI Words: "Delve", "tapestry", "game-changer", "unlock", "revolutionize".
