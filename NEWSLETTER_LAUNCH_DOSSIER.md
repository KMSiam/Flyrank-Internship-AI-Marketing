Most websites only implement basic Organization schema. But Perplexity and SearchGPT don't crawl the web to find your company address—they crawl to extract high-confidence answers to buyer prompts.

### 1. THE HARD DATA: The 18.9% Age Cliff
In FlyRank’s latest empirical study analyzing 120,700 commercial and editorial URLs (state-of-seo-2026.flyrank.ai), we compared pages that gained organic traffic against pages actively bleeding rank.

The surprising finding? Word count was almost identical:
• Growing pages: 1,487 words average
• Declining pages: 1,481 words average (a 6-word difference)

The metric deciding ranking momentum was age. Growing pages were 18.9% younger (185 days vs. 228 days). 

The Takeaway: Stop paying freelance writers $400 for new 3,000-word blog posts. Content decay occurs because after ~185 days without an entity refresh, search algorithms begin testing fresher sources. Reallocate 50% of your editorial bandwidth to updating pages Google already knows.

---

### 2. THE PLAYBOOK: The 50-Word Direct Answer Rule
How do you get Google AI Overviews and Perplexity to pull your site into their summary cards?

LLMs operate on Information Density. They penalize long preamble paragraphs like "In today's fast-paced digital world..."

Here is the exact layout formula that wins citations:
1. Turn your H2 into an exact-match conversational query (e.g., "What is the best AI SEO tool for B2B SaaS?").
2. Put the direct, objective answer in the first 50 to 80 words directly beneath the heading.
3. Follow with a structured 3-column comparison table.
4. Wrap the section in verified JSON-LD FAQ schema.

When an LLM web crawler evaluates that page, the answer is immediately extractable without parsing through fluff.
