Hook: Before you pay an agency another $3,000 for "10x skyscraper blog posts," look at this number: 1,487 vs. 1,481.

We just audited 120,700 live URLs. That’s the average word count of pages gaining Google traffic versus pages actively dying. A 6-word difference. Word count is completely irrelevant.
