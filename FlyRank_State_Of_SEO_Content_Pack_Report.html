3. High-Impact Asset Details

Blog 1: The 18.9% Age Gap

Analyzes the 43-day tipping point between growing (185 days) and declining (228 days) content across 120,700 URLs. Shows that declining pages still accumulate impressions but lose click share because search engines prioritize fresher documents citing updated statistics. Outlines a 3-step programmatic refresh playbook.

Blog 2: The 1.06% AI Referral Tipping Point

Breaks down 17,693 direct sessions originating from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini. Highlights that Perplexity and ChatGPT command over 84% of conversational referrals, and that AI search traffic converts at 3.2x the rate of traditional search due to high user verification intent.

Video Script Highlights
