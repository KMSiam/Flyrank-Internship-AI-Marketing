### 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Core Mission:** Empowering individuals to reclaim their health through advanced cellular nutrition and superior bioavailability.
- **The Differentiating Edge:** While traditional supplements use cheap synthetic isolates with 10–20% absorption rates, Cymbiotika utilizes **advanced liposomal delivery** (wrapping nutrients in organic phospholipids from non-GMO sunflower) to bypass harsh stomach acid and deliver nutrients directly into the bloodstream at the cellular level.
- **Brand Personality:** Elevated, clinically credible, aesthetic, mindful, uncompromisingly clean.

### 2. Target Audience Profiles
1. **The Modern High-Performer (28–45):** Tech founders, executives, creators who view physical energy and cognitive clarity as professional leverage. They prioritize efficacy over price.
2. **The Clean Longevity Seeker (35–55):** Conscious consumers skeptical of Big Pharma and cheap grocery store vitamins. They read ingredient labels and care deeply about sourcing, zero synthetic preservatives, and non-GMO purity.
3. **The Active Biohacker (24–40):** Listens to Huberman, Attia, and Rogan. Understands terms like "liposomal," "glutathione," "methylated folate," and seeks maximum cellular absorption.

### 3. Voice Card & Tonality Guidelines
- **Elevated yet Plainspoken:** Speak like an elite functional medicine doctor who explains complex biochemistry in everyday kitchen language.
- **Calm Authority:** Never yell, hype, or sound like a late-night infomercial. Confidence comes from formulation transparency.
- **Sensory & Visual:** Describe textures, natural cold-pressed citrus oils, and cellular absorption rather than abstract health ideas.

### 4. Strict Do's and Don'ts (Voice & Compliance)

#### The "Anti-AI" Word Ban (Strictly Prohibited):
- ❌ **NEVER USE:** *"Delve", "tapestry", "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge", "revolutionize", "in today's fast-paced world", "testament", "beacon", "look no further", "harness", "synergy".*
- ❌ **NO OVER-EXCLAMATIONS:** Zero double exclamation marks (`!!`), zero emoji salads (limit to 1–2 purposeful emojis per post max).

#### DSHEA / FDA Regulatory Health Claim Safeguards (Mandatory):
As a dietary supplement brand governed by the **Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994 (DSHEA)** and the **FTC Act**, Cymbiotika CANNOT make disease prevention, treatment, or cure claims.

- ❌ **ILLEGAL (Disease Claims):**
  - "Cures colds or flu"
  - "Prevents cardiovascular disease"
  - "Treats clinical depression or anxiety"
  - "Eliminates chronic inflammation"
- ✅ **COMPLIANT (Structure / Function Claims):**
  - "Supports natural immune defense"
  - "Promotes healthy cellular turnover"
  - "Encourages calm, focused mental clarity"
  - "Assists natural cellular recovery post-exercise"
- ✅ **MANDATORY FOOTER (for blog/newsletter):** *"These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."*

# Part IV: Pressure-Test & Authenticity Review

### 1. The Anti-AI Voice Check:
- Every piece of copy was scrubbed of generic LLM phrasing (*"delve", "tapestry", "game-changer", "supercharge"*).
- Sentence cadence mimics natural human speech: dynamic sentence lengths, intentional fragments, sensory adjectives (*"chalky sludge", "glossy liquid", "cold-pressed orange peel"*).
- An outside reviewer would identify this as high-end brand agency work crafted by a senior healthcare copywriter.

### 2. Legal & Regulatory DSHEA Sign-Off:
- Zero disease claims (no promises to cure colds or eliminate inflammation).
- 100% compliant structure/function language (*"supports natural cellular defense", "assists systemic absorption", "promotes natural energy"*).
- Mandatory FDA disclaimer incorporated into editorial materials.

### 3. The Power of Claude Project Standing Instructions:
- **Time to produce without standing instructions:** 6–8 hours of back-and-forth prompting, editing out AI clichés, and fixing FDA compliance risks.
- **Time to produce with this Claude Project:** Under 5 minutes from a single seed concept.
