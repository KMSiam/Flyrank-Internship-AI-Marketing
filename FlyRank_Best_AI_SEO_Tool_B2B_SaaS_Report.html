1. Why B2B SaaS Organic Traffic Is Decoupling from Google

For years, B2B SaaS growth relied on an unvarying playbook: find mid-tail informational keywords, produce a 2,500-word skyscraper article, build a handful of backlinks, and collect organic trial signups. In 2026, that formula has broken down. Between Google’s aggressive rollout of AI Overviews (AIO) capturing zero-click searches and technical buyers migrating software evaluations into ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude, traditional rankings no longer guarantee inbound pipeline.

Recent search studies show that informational queries now face CTRs below 1.2% due to instant generative snapshots. To build predictable revenue, B2B SaaS teams must optimize for two search realities simultaneously: traditional blue-link organic search and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) / Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
