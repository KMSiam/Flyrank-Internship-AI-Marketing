4. Inaugural Launch Issue: Dispatch #001

Subject: Issue #001: The 18.9% age cliff (and who ChatGPT cited this week)
Preview: 120,700 URLs analyzed: why your 2,500-word articles are losing rank after day 185...
THE AUTONOMOUS SEARCH DISPATCH #001 · By KM Siam

1. THE HARD DATA: The 18.9% Age Cliff
Analyzing 120,700 commercial and editorial URLs (state-of-seo-2026.flyrank.ai), growing pages averaged 1,487 words while declining pages averaged 1,481 words. Word count is completely dead as a ranking signal. The separating metric was age: growing pages were 18.9% younger (185 days vs. 228 days). Reallocate 50% of your editorial bandwidth to refreshing existing pages rather than publishing unindexed new posts.

2. THE PLAYBOOK: The 50-Word Direct Answer Rule
LLMs penalize fluffy introductory prose. To win AI Overviews: format H2 as an exact conversational question, deliver the direct objective answer in the first 50–80 words, follow with a 3-column table, and encode with JSON-LD FAQPage schema.

3. THE LIVE TEARDOWN: Praella's 5.2x Search Lead Surge
Praella deployed FlyRank's autonomous refresh architecture across 45 high-impression URLs, resulting in a 5.2x surge in inbound demo requests and a tripled pipeline without hiring additional sales reps.
