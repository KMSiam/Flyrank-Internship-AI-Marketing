## 1. How the LinkedIn Algorithm Decides What Gets Shown

LinkedIn's feed distribution algorithm in 2026 operates on a multi-stage scoring architecture designed to filter out spam and prioritize high-value professional knowledge exchange:

```

```

### The 4 Core Signals the Algorithm Rewards:

1. **Dwell Time (The Primary Ranking Signal):**
   - Introduced and expanded by LinkedIn Engineering, dwell time measures two metrics: **dwell time on the feed** (seconds spent viewing the post while scrolling) and **dwell time on the click/carousel** (seconds spent expanding "see more" and consuming the asset).
   - A user spending 45+ seconds on a post signals high information density, elevating the post to 2nd- and 3rd-degree network feeds.

2. **Comment Depth & Reply Velocity ("The Conversation Engine"):**
   - Not all engagements are equal. Reactions (Like, Celebrate, Insightful) have a relative weight of 1x.
   - Comments with **15+ words** carry **4x to 6x the weight** of a generic like.
   - Author replies to comments within the "Golden Hour" (first 60 minutes) trigger an algorithmic multiplier, re-injecting the post into active feeds.

3. **Multi-Slide Document Carousel Swipes:**
   - PDF slide decks uploaded as native documents are treated as active interactive media.
   - Every slide swipe is registered by LinkedIn telemetry as an engagement event, multiplying dwell time and engagement scores.

4. **Saves & Thoughtful Reposts:**
   - "Save for later" actions indicate lasting educational or tactical utility.
   - Reposts with thoughts carry significantly more distribution weight than blind "Instant Reposts."

## 2. The Short-Form vs. Long-Form Trade-Off on LinkedIn

| Format | Length / Specs | Algorithmic Strength | Algorithmic Weakness | Ideal Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Short-Form Text** | 100–300 characters | Quick consumption; fast scroll speed. | Low dwell time; often buried by the feed unless it achieves massive comment velocity. | Quick contrarian takes, questions, or poll prompts. |
| **Long-Form Text** | 1,200–1,800 characters | High dwell time; triggers "see more" expansion; ample space for structured frameworks. | Requires exceptional copywriting; walls of text without line breaks cause instant bounce. | Tactical breakdowns, step-by-step playbooks, founder stories. |
| **Document Carousel (PDF)** | 7–12 slides (1080x1350) | **Highest dwell time on LinkedIn**; swipe mechanics generate recursive engagement pings. | High production effort; requires tight visual design and strong typography. | Data benchmark summaries, visual teardowns, step-by-step guides. |
| **Native Video** | 60–90 seconds | Video auto-play grabs visual attention; rich personality signal. | Subtitles mandatory (85% watch muted); lower save and share rate compared to PDF documents. | Product walkthroughs, founder interview clips, conference keynotes. |

**The Strategic Choice for FlyRank:** A hybrid rhythm of **Long-Form Text Breakdown Posts** (1,200–1,500 characters) and **PDF Document Carousels** (8–10 slides). This maximizes dwell time, encourages saves, and delivers empirical benchmark data without losing executive attention.

## 3. Algorithmic Penalties, Shadowbans & Compliance Limits

### The 4 Penalties That Kill Reach on LinkedIn:
1. **The External Link Penalty (The "Platform Walled Garden"):**
   - Placing an external link in the main post body incurs a severe **40% to 50% reach demotion**. LinkedIn wants users to stay on LinkedIn.
   - *Algorithmic Solution:* Deliver 100% of the standalone value natively in the post, and place destination links in the **pinned first comment** or in the author's profile link.
2. **The "Edit Within 15 Minutes" Penalty:**
   - Editing a post immediately after publishing resets its micro-testing engagement score and can stall algorithmic distribution.
   - *Solution:* Proofread thoroughly before hitting publish; avoid immediate edits.
3. **Engagement Pod Detection & Tag Spamming:**
   - Tagging 10+ people who do not immediately respond, or using artificial reciprocal comment pods, triggers LinkedIn’s automated spam classification, resulting in aggressive shadowbanning.
   - *Solution:* Tag only 1–2 individuals directly cited in the content who will actively engage.
4. **Formatting Walls of Text:**
   - Long dense paragraphs without line breaks cause immediate scroll-past, registering negative dwell time (<1.5 seconds) and halting distribution.

### Niche Compliance Rules (AI Software & Performance Guarantees):
- **FTC Truth in Advertising on AI Claims:** Avoid unsubstantiated absolutes like *"guarantees #1 ranking on Google and Perplexity in 24 hours"*. All performance numbers must cite verifiable case studies (e.g. Praella’s 5.2x growth or the 120,700 URL benchmark study).
- **AI Tool Transparency:** Explicitly delineate human editorial judgment from automated AI data extraction to build enterprise credibility.
